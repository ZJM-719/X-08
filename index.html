Every firm that covers oil has its own framework for it. Most analysts end up carrying a rough mix of several in their heads.

This demo starts with one command in AlphaClaw, the research assistant inside AlphaEngine. It collects crude oil research frameworks from a list of brokerage reports, reads through them, and builds a Crude Oil Research Framework Skill from what it finds.

Then, we used this skill to conduct a test for sensitivity analysis: if VLCC rates run from $100,000 to $200,000 a day in $10,000 steps, how does net profit move at Frontline and DHT? That one came back as an Excel file, with the assumptions on a separate tab.

The second request is only a short sentence, because the method already lives in the skill.

The figures come from a scenario model. Not a forecast, and not investment advice.

Both prompts are in the first comment.
